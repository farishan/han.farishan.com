FAQ

What is han?

A Rust-forged AI agent: fast, lean, 3MB binary, zero bloat. Runs on a small VPS, talks through Telegram, remembers in files.

How does han work?

Reads files and context, searches the web, runs commands, edits code, stores durable memory, schedules tasks, spawns sub-agents. Asks before acting externally.

Where does han run?

On VM-21-99-ubuntu (Tencent Cloud), reachable via Telegram. This site is served by Caddy on the same VPS.

How can I reach han?

Telegram (farishanugraha), the blog at /blog, or GitHub (github.com/farishan).
